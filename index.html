Our Philosophy

A Place to Stay Awhile

The bruine kroeg — the brown café — has been the beating heart of Dutch neighborhoods since the 17th century. Named for the tobacco-stained walls and centuries of warm breath, these are not bars. They are living rooms for the city.

De Bruine Kroon brings that spirit to Little Rock: no televisions, no bottle service, no neon. Just good Belgian and Dutch draft, bitterballen on the bar, and the rare pleasure of nowhere else to be.

  • 12 rotating Dutch & Belgian taps — Heineken, Grolsch, Duvel, Westmalle & more
  • Jenever (Dutch gin) alongside Arkansas-sourced whiskeys
  • Traditional Dutch bar snacks: bitterballen, kaasblokjes, haring
  • No reservations. First come, first seated at the long oak bar
  • Cash & card — but regulars get a running tab

The Experience

What Kind of Place Is This?

🪵

Dark Oak & Patina

Reclaimed Dutch oak bar top. Walls of warm amber plaster, deliberately aged. Brass fixtures that catch candlelight. The kind of room that makes everyone look interesting.

🕯️

No Screens. No DJs.

A vinyl record player in the corner. Jazz, blues, and the occasional Dutch folk record. Conversation is the entertainment. If you need a screen, there is one, and it shows football — only football.

🪑

Long Tables & Short Distances

Community seating at long wooden benches. A row of bar stools facing the taps. You may arrive alone and leave with new opinions about Dutch history.

📰

Papers on the Bar

The Arkansas Democrat-Gazette. The NRC Handelsblad (yes, the Dutch paper). A worn stack of paperbacks available to borrow. Nobody will rush you.

🌿

Dutch Details

Delft tile above the bar. A vintage ANWB road map of the Netherlands framed on the wall. Original De Stijl prints. A canal scene in oil, probably fake, definitely beloved.

🌊

A Patio Facing the River

A small terrace overlooking the Arkansas River — the closest thing to a Dutch canal we could find. Heated lamps in winter. No umbrella tables. If it rains, come inside.

The House Rules

We have only a few, and they are not suggestions.

I
No Shouting Raise your voice only in celebration of a goal, a good joke, or an unexpectedly good round of bitterballen.
II
Order at the Bar There is no table service. Walk to the bar. Talk to the barman. This is the original social contract.
III
Dogs Welcome, No Children After 7pm A well-behaved dog is an asset to any bruine café. Well-behaved children are welcome until 7pm — after that, it belongs to the grown-ups.
IV
Your Phone is for Calls We are not a content opportunity. You may photograph your first kopstoot. Once. Then put it away.
V
Regulars Get Tabs After your third visit, ask the barman for a tab. After your tenth, he will know your order before you sit down.
VI
Last Call is Last Call We close at 2am on weekends. We will not be persuaded otherwise. We have heard it all.
